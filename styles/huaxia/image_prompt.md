# 画风提示模板：恢弘历史写实

统一色调：**暖金 + 青灰**（golden hour 侧光、青灰天空/水面），低饱和，胶片颗粒，电影宽容度。全片同一套前缀，保证一致。

## 固定前缀
```
Vertical 9:16, epic cinematic historical realism, ancient China ({朝代}), unified color grade: warm gold highlights and teal-grey shadows, low saturation, soft film grain, volumetric light, matte painting scale, authentic {朝代} costume, architecture and tools, no text, no letters, no watermark, no modern objects, main subject in upper two-thirds, calmer lower third for subtitles.
```

## 景别后缀（与 STORYBOARD.md 的 type 对应，相邻镜不同）
- portrait: `close-up portrait of {人物}, determined expression, rim light`
- wide: `vast aerial establishing shot of {场景}, tiny human figures for scale`
- object: `macro close-up of {物件}, shallow depth of field`
- action: `dynamic moment of {动作}, motion, dust and water spray`
- detail: `atmospheric detail of {细节}, quiet mood`
- card: 不生图，用 `cards.py`（theme=paper 与本风格统一）

## 禁止
真实在世人物肖像、可读文字、地图上的现代国界、夸张奇幻元素（龙、神光）除非文案明确是传说。
